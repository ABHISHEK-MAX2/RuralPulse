KARNATAKA RURAL HEALTHCARE — 5-LAYER SYNTHETIC PROTOTYPE
750 synthetic village records.

IMPORTANT: All records are synthetic for a hackathon/demo. DEMO-KA-* codes are NOT LGD codes.
Layer 5 is explicitly SYNTHETIC_DEMO. Vulnerability_Score/Risk_Level/Intervention are
prototype outputs, not government scores or real-time government records.

LAYER 1: Administrative geography — District, Taluk, Village, demo codes.
LAYER 2: Demographic/infrastructure — population, literacy, roads, water, electricity.
LAYER 3: Health — facility availability, PHC distance, immunization, ANC, delivery, malnutrition.
LAYER 4: Weather/hazard — rainfall, anomaly, heavy-rainfall and isolation risk.
LAYER 5: Operational demo — vaccine/IFA/medicine stock, doctor attendance, MMU, outreach recency.

JOIN KEY: Village_Code

For the final project, replace Layer 1 demo codes with actual LGD identifiers and replace
real-data layers with verified official sources where available.
